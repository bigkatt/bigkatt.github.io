Privacy Policy

Wormzy Galaxy: Idle Space RPG (iOS and Android app com.bigkatt.wormzygalaxy) · Last updated: November 27, 2026

In short: Wormzy Galaxy: Idle Space RPG saves your game on your own device, and you can play without an account. If you choose to sign in (for example with Sign in with Apple), a copy of your game is stored with Google Firebase so you can load it on another device, and online features such as the Arena, Armadas, leaderboards and chat use your pilot name and game stats. The game shows optional ads through Google AdMob and sells optional items through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We don't sell your personal information.

Who we are

Wormzy Galaxy: Idle Space RPG is made by an independent developer, Wormzy Gaming ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, email us at wormzygaming@gmail.com.

Information the game keeps on your device

Your progress (stages, ships, gear, cards, currencies, settings, your pilot name, avatar, frame, nameplate and title) is stored in the app's storage on your phone or tablet. Without signing in, it is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it from your device.

Signing in and cloud save (optional)

Signing in is optional. When you choose Sign in with Apple (or, on Android, Sign in with Google):

  • Google Firebase Authentication receives a sign-in token and gives you a Wormzy Galaxy account ID. With Sign in with Apple you can choose Hide My Email, in which case we only receive a private relay address from Apple.
  • A copy of your saved game is stored in Google Cloud Firestore in the United States, with the time it was saved and a random ID for the device it came from. Only you, signed in, can change it.
  • The game backs up regularly while you play so you can load the same game on any device you sign in on.

We use this only to keep your game linked to you. You can sign out at any time (your game stays on the device) or delete your account and cloud save from inside the game (see Delete your data).

Online features: Arena, Armadas, leaderboards

When online play is available and you are signed in, other players can see your pilot name, avatar, frame, nameplate, title, Battle Rating, ship and stats, your Arena and leaderboard rank, and your Armada and its activity. Arena battles use a saved snapshot of your ship, so other players can fight it while you are away. Please don't use your real full name as your pilot name.

Until online play is turned on, the other pilots shown in the Arena, Armadas, rankings and chat are computer-controlled, and anything you type in chat stays on your device.

Chat

When live chat is available, messages you send in World or Armada chat are stored on our Firebase servers and shown to other players with your pilot name and title. To keep chat safe:

  • messages are checked by an automatic filter, which may use an automated moderation service, to block offensive content, links and personal information;
  • players can report a message and block a player; reported messages, the reporter and the sender are reviewed by our moderators;
  • we may mute or ban players who break the rules, and we keep reports and moderation actions for as long as needed to keep the game safe.

Never share personal information (your address, phone number, passwords or real name) in chat. You can turn chat off in the game's settings.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch one to get a bonus. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking when asked) and other device identifiers
  • approximate location, based on your IP address
  • information about how you interact with ads, plus crash and performance data

Google uses this data as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. On iPhone you can change your tracking choice in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Players in the European Economic Area and the UK are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. Players who own the Ad-Free Pass are not shown ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The store tells us that a purchase was made so the game can give you the item. Purchases are covered by Apple's and Google's own privacy policies.

Game statistics and crash reports

To fix problems and balance the game, the game may use Google Firebase services to collect crash reports and basic, non-identifying game events (for example how far players get and which features are used), together with device information such as the phone model, system version, app version, language and approximate country. This data is used only to improve the game and is not sold.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game can remind you when something in the game is ready. These reminders are scheduled on your device; no personal data is sent to us to make them. You can turn them off in your device's settings.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We don't sell your personal information or use your cloud save for advertising.

Children

Wormzy Galaxy: Idle Space RPG is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Delete your data

  • Game on your device: uninstall the app.
  • Account, cloud save and online data: in the game, open your Pilot menu → Account → Delete account. This deletes your sign-in, cloud save and online profile.
  • Or email wormzygaming@gmail.com from any address with your pilot name and account ID, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have extra rights to access, correct or delete your data; email us and we'll help.

Changes to this policy

If the game adds new features that use data, we will update this page and change the date at the top before those features go live.
