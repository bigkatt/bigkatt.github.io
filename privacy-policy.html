Privacy Policy

Wild Hunter: Idle Hunt (Android app com.bigkatt.wildhunter) · Last updated: November 20, 2026

In short: Wild Hunter saves your game only on your own device. We don't ask for your name, email or any account. The game shows optional ads through Google AdMob, sells optional items through Google Play, and uses Google Firebase to count how the game is played and to report crashes. Those Google services collect some data, as explained below.

Who we are

Wild Hunter: Idle Hunt is made by an independent developer, Wormzy Gaming ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, email us at wormzygaming@gmail.com.

Information the game keeps on your device

Your progress (stages, gear, pets, settings, your hunter's name and look) is stored only in the app's storage on your phone or tablet. It is not sent to us or to any server we run. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Reset in the game, deletes it.

The hunter name you type is only shown inside the game on your device. Please don't use your real full name.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch one to get a bonus. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • approximate location, based on your IP address
  • information about how you interact with ads, plus crash and performance data

Google uses this data as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Players in the European Economic Area and the UK are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Players who buy "No Ads" are not shown ads.

Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google tells us that a purchase was made so the game can give you the item. Google's handling of payment data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Game statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To learn how players use the game (for example how far players get, which features they open and how long a session lasts) and to fix crashes, the game uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. These collect:

  • an app instance ID (a random number for this install) and your device's advertising ID
  • game events, such as reaching a stage, opening a window, finishing a purchase or watching an ad
  • device and app information, such as the phone model, Android version, app version and language
  • approximate location (country or region), based on your IP address
  • crash and error details, such as what the game was doing when it stopped

This data is used only to improve and fix the game. It isn't used to identify you, and it isn't sold. Google's handling is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase. You can opt out of personalized advertising using your device's ad settings, as described above.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game can remind you when a Workshop upgrade or an egg is ready, or when your hunter's bag is full. These reminders are scheduled on your device by the game itself; no data is sent to us to make them. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

Discord and links

The game's "Report a bug" button opens our Discord server in your browser or the Discord app. Anything you post there is handled by Discord under the Discord Privacy Policy. Error reports you choose to copy from the game contain the game version and what went wrong, not personal information.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • The game has no player-to-player chat at this time. Other hunters shown in leaderboards, parties and the chat bar are computer-controlled, and anything you type there stays on your device.

Children

Wild Hunter is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices and rights

Because your saved game stays on your device, you control it: you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or resetting the game. For data collected by Google, use your device's ad settings or Google's data and privacy controls. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have extra rights to access or delete data; email us and we'll help. Step-by-step instructions are on our Delete your data page.

Changes to this policy

If the game adds new features that use data, such as online play, cloud saves or chat, we will update this page and change the date at the top before those features go live.
