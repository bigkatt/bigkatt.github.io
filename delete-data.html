Delete your data

Wild Hunter: Idle Hunt · Developer: Wormzy Gaming · Last updated: November 21, 2026

You can delete everything yourself, inside the game. Your game is saved on your device, and also in the cloud if you turned on Cloud save (Google sign-in). To ask us to delete game statistics or crash reports, email wormzygaming@gmail.com.

How to delete your data

  1. Delete your Cloud save and Google sign-in (account): in the game, open ⚙️ Settings → ☁️ Cloud save → Delete cloud save, then confirm. This deletes your saved game from our cloud and your Wild Hunter sign-in straight away. The game on your phone is not touched. If you no longer have the game, email wormzygaming@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, and we will delete it within 30 days.
  2. Delete your saved game: in the game, open ⚙️ Settings → Reset, or uninstall Wild Hunter. Your progress, settings and hunter name are removed from your device straight away.
  3. Ask us to delete game statistics and crash reports: email wormzygaming@gmail.com (wormzygaming@gmail.com) with the subject "Delete my Wild Hunter data". Tell us roughly when you played and on what phone model, so we can find your data. We will reply within 30 days.
  4. Ad data: ads are provided by Google AdMob. Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads), and use Google's data and privacy controls.

What is deleted

  • Your cloud save (your game progress, hunter name, save time and phone ID) and your Wild Hunter sign-in (Google email address, name and user ID): deleted straight away when you use Delete cloud save, or within 30 days when you email us.
  • Your saved game (stages, gear, pets, settings, hunter name): deleted from your device when you reset or uninstall.
  • Game statistics from Google Firebase Analytics linked to your install: deleted when you ask us.
  • Crash reports from Firebase Crashlytics linked to your install: deleted when you ask us.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Weekly safety backups of the cloud database may still hold a deleted cloud save until those backups expire (never more than 14 weeks), then they are deleted automatically.
  • Firebase Analytics data is kept for up to 2 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Records of purchases are kept by Google Play, not by us, under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Statistics that have been combined so they can't identify anyone (for example "how many players reached Wolf Ridge") may be kept.
